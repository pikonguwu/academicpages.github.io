<section class="home-section home__shell" id="research" aria-labelledby="research-title">
  <header class="section-heading" data-reveal>
    <h2 id="research-title">Research Interests</h2>
    <p class="section-heading__copy">My long-term goal is to build intelligent systems that understand multimodal context and collaborate naturally with people in the physical world.</p>
  </header>
  <div class="research-list">
    <article data-reveal>
      <h3>Vision-Language-Action</h3>
      <p>Connecting perception, language understanding, and physical control in unified robot learning systems.</p>
    </article>
    <article data-reveal>
      <h3>Embodied AI</h3>
      <p>Agents that learn through perception, reasoning, and interaction with the physical world.</p>
    </article>
    <article data-reveal>
      <h3>Human-Robot Interaction</h3>
      <p>Creating intuitive, adaptive, and trustworthy collaboration between humans and robots.</p>
    </article>
    <article data-reveal>
      <h3>Multimodal Models</h3>
      <p>Learning representations that align visual, linguistic, and action signals for general-purpose intelligence.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
