MSNav combines a dynamic map memory, spatial reasoning, and LLM-based decision making to address memory overload, weak spatial reasoning, and cross-modal grounding in zero-shot vision-and-language navigation.

**Personal contribution:** Co-first author; responsible for the experimental implementation and part of the paper writing.
