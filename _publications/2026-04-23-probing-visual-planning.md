We present EAR, an editing-as-reasoning paradigm that reformulates visual planning as a single-step image transformation, and AMAZE, a procedurally generated dataset covering Maze and Queen problems.

**Personal contribution:** First author and project leader at BIGAI (October 2025 - April 2026). Built the Amaze benchmark and designed a fully automated, rule-based image-to-image evaluation pipeline for visual spatial reasoning and planning.
